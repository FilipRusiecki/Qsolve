QSolve has significant experience in the delivery of healthcare projects across Ireland, providing commercial and quantity surveying services on both capital developments and critical infrastructure upgrade programmes within live healthcare environments.
A flagship project within our portfolio is the Central Regional Clinic in Waterford, where we acted as Lead Quantity Surveyor on behalf of the client, providing full commercial management and cost control services throughout the project lifecycle.
Building on this experience, QSolve is currently appointed on a major nationwide healthcare infrastructure programme involving hospitals and healthcare campuses across Ireland. This programme includes the upgrade and replacement of emergency lighting systems, fire detection and alarm systems, and campus-wide Wi-Fi infrastructure to improve safety, compliance, and operational resilience across the healthcare estate.
Our experience working within live healthcare environments gives us a detailed understanding of the challenges associated with phased delivery, maintaining uninterrupted patient services, stakeholder engagement, and managing complex compliance requirements. We provide practical commercial solutions that support the successful delivery of critical healthcare infrastructure while ensuring value for money and minimal disruption to clinical operations.
