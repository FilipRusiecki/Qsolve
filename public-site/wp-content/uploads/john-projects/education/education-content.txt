As Managing  Director of QSolve, John Conroy brings over 30 years of construction industry experience, including a number of years in senior leadership roles within the Irish education sector acting directly on behalf of the Department of Education as a client-facing Quantity Surveyor and commercial advisor. John has held lead QS responsibilities across several of the Department's flagship programmes, including the Schools Design & Build Framework, the National Schools Fire Remediation Programme and the Special Educational Needs (SEN) Modular Accommodation Programme. Working at the forefront of project delivery, he has provided commercial leadership, procurement advice, cost management, tender evaluation and strategic financial oversight across projects ranging from individual classroom extensions to major multi-school programmes throughout the country. His extensive experience working directly with Department representatives, design teams and contractors has given him a detailed understanding of public sector procurement, framework delivery and the successful delivery of educational infrastructure within live operational environments.
