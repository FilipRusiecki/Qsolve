DUB 11 Data Centre
QSolve provided commercial and quantity surveying support on the DUB11 Data Centre Refurbishment project in Dublin, a complex demolition and enabling works package within a live operational data centre campus. The project involved the strip-out of existing data hall infrastructure, coordination of extensive mechanical and electrical decommissioning works, management of structural alterations and floor strengthening works, and the commercial administration of variations, change control, and final account negotiations. Working closely with the main contractor and project stakeholders, QSolve provided practical commercial advice to ensure that additional works, programme impacts, and specialist de-energisation activities were properly identified, valued, and managed throughout the project lifecycle.
