Fire Remediation
QSolve has developed extensive expertise in the delivery of complex fire remediation and life safety upgrade projects across residential, healthcare and occupied buildings. They are currently acting as lead commercial advisor on the Crescent Building, Park West, a major Housing Agency Pathfinder project comprising a 232-apartment residential development across nine cores and seven floors. The project involves extensive internal and external fire remediation, compliance upgrades and energy retrofit measures to bring the development in line with current fire safety and building performance standards, with reporting provided directly to the Housing Agency throughout the programme. In the healthcare sector, John is also supporting the fire remediation and upgrade of two live nursing home environments in Ballygunner, Waterford, and Kilcummn, Killarney, where works are being carefully phased and managed to maintain the continued operation of the facilities and minimise disruption to residents and staff. His experience in occupied buildings and live operational environments provides clients with confidence that critical compliance works can be delivered safely, efficiently and with minimal impact on building users.
